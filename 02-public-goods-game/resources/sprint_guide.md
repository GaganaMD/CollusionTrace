## 1. Judging Rubric (15 Points Total)

### Dimension 1: Impact Potential & Innovation (1–5)
* **1 (Negligible):** No clear problem addressed, or no meaningful novelty.
* **2 (Limited):** Addresses a real problem with a generic or well-trodden approach. Incremental at best.
* **3 (Moderate):** Clear problem with a reasonable approach; some novelty in framing or method.
* **4 (Significant):** Important problem with an original approach, or neglected problem area. Valuable contribution.
* **5 (Exceptional):** Critical problem with genuinely novel approach, or opens new research direction. Clear theory of change.

### Dimension 2: Execution Quality (1–5)
* **1 (Seriously flawed):** Broken methodology, uninterpretable results, or failed implementation.
* **2 (Weak):** Significant gaps: missing validation, flawed experimental design, or incomplete implementation.
* **3 (Competent):** Solid given short duration. Sound methodology, interpretable results, acknowledged limitations, clear conclusions.
* **4 (Strong):** Thorough methodology with convincing validation. Results clearly support conclusions.
* **5 (Exceptional):** Ambitious scope executed rigorously. Surprising findings, novel methods, or unusually robust validation.

### Dimension 3: Presentation & Clarity (1–5)
* **1 (Incomprehensible):** Cannot determine claims or methodology.
* **2 (Hard to follow):** Key information buried, missing, or diluted by excessive length.
* **3 (Clear enough):** Core content clearly present: problem, method, findings, limitations.
* **4 (Well presented):** Easy to follow, well-structured, appropriate level of detail.
* **5 (Exceptionally clear):** A pleasure to read. Complex ideas made accessible.

## 2. Submission Deliverables & Requirements

### Required:
1. **Research Report (PDF):** 4 to 8 pages using the [official submission template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Y5wd3VRbXU5N08BvAGiJUQTaWbMCSPjhzXsR-ozuehg/copy?usp=sharing).
2. **Project Title & Abstract:** Abstract must be 150 words or fewer.
3. **Authors & Affiliations:** Listed clearly.
4. **Required Appendix:** *"Limitations and Dual-Use / Ethical Considerations"* (Mandatory: state what the result does and does not prove, and whether findings could inadvertently aid collusion or hinder detection).

### Optional:
* Public GitHub repository link.
* 3 to 5-minute video presentation / walkthrough.
* Presentation slides (PDF/PPT up to 25 MB).
