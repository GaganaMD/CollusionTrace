# What placed at Apart sprints (2025-2026): observations only (2026-10-19)
Data: notes/apart_winners_data_20261019.json (86 projects, 12 events, ~2 public reviews each). Delegate-extracted; spot-checked 2 of 3 pages (1 failed to load, 1 attribution corrected).
Limits: no numeric scores public; non-placed set small (26) and chosen by delegates; AIxBio rows are search-snippet only (low confidence); CBRN 2025 sprint published no projects.
## Findings
- Rigor checklist items (code, baselines, limitations section, ablation) occur at similar rates in placed and non-placed projects; they are table stakes, not separators.
- Reviewer text for placed projects more often praises controls/baselines (21% vs 6% of strengths) and clarity (49% vs 39%); n is small.
- Placed work is often a reframing with one headline lesson, an evaluator stress-test or decodable-but-not-causal result, paired/inverted controls, and honest null results; reviewers still flag small n, single seeds, missing FPR on winners.
- Null/negative results can place (Syscon, Brazil job displacement); a strong methodological null did not (Probes Detect the Instruction).
## Speakers (stated, from their papers)
Text-only monitoring insufficient; talk vs action diverge (Colosseum, When Agents Lie); critique of one-shot, homogeneous, single-scheme and judge-only evaluations; self-reported plans are not ground truth; no shared benchmark (StegoBench, Lee). Judges are not publicly named.
